Community terms & conditions

By joining the HV Petvilla CommuniTree community (via WhatsApp or Instagram), you agree to the following terms. Please read carefully.

1. Community Purpose

CommuniTree is a space for pet parents in Hyderabad to share knowledge, experiences, and support related to pet care — for every kind of legal pet.

2. Code of Conduct

  1. Be respectful and kind to all members at all times.
  2. No hate speech, harassment, bullying, or offensive language.
  3. No sharing of graphic, violent, or disturbing content involving animals.
  4. No promotion of illegal pet trade, wildlife smuggling, or cruelty.
  5. Advertising and spam are not permitted without prior permission from HV Petvilla admins.

3. Content Sharing

  1. You may share pet photos, videos, care tips, and questions relevant to the group topic.
  2. By sharing content, you grant HV Petvilla permission to re-share it on our official social media channels, with credit to you.
  3. Do not share personal details (phone numbers, home addresses) of others without their consent.

4. Health & Veterinary Advice

Information shared in the community is for general guidance only and does not replace professional veterinary advice. Always consult a qualified vet for your pet's health concerns.

5. Privacy

Do not share screenshots of private conversations from the group to public platforms without all parties' consent.

6. Moderation & Removal

HV Petvilla admins reserve the right to remove any member who violates these terms, without prior notice.

7. Changes to Terms

HV Petvilla may update these terms at any time. Continued membership of the community implies acceptance of any updated terms.

For questions, contact us via the contact page or on WhatsApp.
