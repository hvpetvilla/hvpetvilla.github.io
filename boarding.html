HV Petvilla offers boarding for all legal pets in Hyderabad — dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, reptiles, hamsters, mice, rats, guinea pigs, sugar gliders and more — for pet parents who travel and want their pet looked after by people who genuinely understand them.
