Pet Boarding

Book a safe, caring slot for your small or exotic pet while you travel. Daily photo updates guaranteed.

HV Petvilla offers dedicated boarding for exotic and small pets in Hyderabad, including birds, rabbits, hamsters, mice, rats and sugar gliders, for pet parents who travel and don't want to leave a hard-to-place pet with an unfamiliar sitter. Every stay includes daily photo updates so you can check in from anywhere, plus any special diet or care notes you share when booking. We're based in Nizampet, Hyderabad, and take bookings for single overnight stays or longer trips.

What's included in boarding

  • Fresh food and water throughout the day
  • Daily WhatsApp photo and video updates
  • Clean, temperature-appropriate enclosures
  • Playtime and social interaction (for suitable pets)
  • Species-specific diet and care expertise

What to bring along

  • Your pet's regular food (especially if on a specific diet)
  • A familiar toy, bedding or blanket for comfort
  • Any medications with clear dosage instructions
  • Vaccination records (if applicable)
