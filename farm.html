Our story

The same care, now on the farm.

For years we've looked after Hyderabad's pets like family. HV Petvilla Farm brings that same patience to our flock of country hens — no cramped cages, no rush, just healthy birds living the way country hens should.

That shows in the taste. Naatu kodi meat is firmer and full of flavour, and country eggs have rich, deep-coloured yolks — the kind you remember from your grandmother's village.

How we raise them

Happy hens, better food.

Good eggs and good meat start with how a bird lives. Here's what every hen on our farm gets.

  1. Room to roam

    Open ground to forage, scratch and dust-bathe — not a cage.

  2. Natural feed

    Grains, greens and whatever they forage — the way country hens have always eaten.

  3. Daily care

    The same team that cares for our boarders checks the flock's health every day.

  4. Collected fresh

    Eggs are gathered and sorted fresh, so they reach you clean and uncracked.
