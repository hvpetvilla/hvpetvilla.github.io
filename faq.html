Frequently Asked Questions

Everything pet parents ask us about boarding, pricing, the shop and CommuniTree — answered.

Boarding
Which pets do you board?
We board most legal pets other than dogs and cats — birds, turtles, tortoises, iguanas, spiders, mice, rats, hamsters, guinea pigs, rabbits and more. We aren't able to accept pets with existing health issues, unless the condition is a physical disability rather than an illness. If you're unsure whether we can take your pet, just ask us.
How much does boarding cost?
Pricing depends on the pet type and whether you'd like us to provide food during the stay. See the Boarding Pricing table for exact rates.
How far in advance should I book?
At least 1 day's notice is enough — book through the form on our Boarding page.
Does the price change if I bring my pet's own food?
Yes — we charge separate rates for boarding with food provided by us versus without. Both rates are listed in the Pricing table.
Will I get updates while my pet is boarding?
Yes — we send a short video update personally to your WhatsApp once a day, so you always know your pet is happy and safe.
What happens if my pet falls sick during boarding?
We're collaborated with a few trusted vets nearby. If your pet falls sick, we'll get them treated by one of our vet partners, and any treatment cost is billed to you separately.
Is there a minimum or maximum boarding duration?
No — there's no minimum or maximum number of days. The boarding amount is payable in advance.
What are your drop-off and pick-up timings?
Weekdays: 7 PM – 10 PM. Weekends: 10 AM – 10 PM.
What's your cancellation policy?
Cancel more than 24 hours before your slot and we'll refund half the advance amount. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the slot time are non-refundable.
Do you accept pets with special needs?
Yes — we take in pets that need special care, including physically challenged pets. We currently can't accept pets that are on ongoing medication.
Shop

Do you deliver outside Hyderabad?
Yes — we deliver anywhere in India, for an additional charge.
What's your return policy?
If an issue with a product is verified as genuine, we offer a full refund.
CommuniTree

CommuniTree is our WhatsApp group where pet parents connect, share opinions and experiences, and support each other. Join here.
Is CommuniTree free to join?
Yes, it's completely free, and open to any pet parent.
