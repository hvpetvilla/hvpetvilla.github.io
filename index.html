HV Petvilla is a Hyderabad-based pet care business run out of Nizampet, offering three things under one roof: overnight boarding for exotic and small pets — birds, rabbits, hamsters, mice, rats and sugar gliders — a curated pet shop for their food and supplies, and CommuniTree, a WhatsApp and Instagram community where local pet parents share advice and support each other. Whether you're travelling and need a trustworthy place to board your pet, restocking supplies, or just looking to connect with other exotic-pet owners nearby, we're a short trip away in Nizampet, Hyderabad, Telangana.
