About us

Caring for Hyderabad's pets, and the people who love them, since 2021.

HV Petvilla is Hyderabad's trusted pet care hub. We offer boarding for all legal pets — from dogs and cats to birds, rabbits, reptiles, hamsters, sugar gliders and more. Alongside boarding, we run a curated pet shop and CommuniTree, a warm community of Hyderabad pet parents on WhatsApp and Instagram.
